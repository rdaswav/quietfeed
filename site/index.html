Three small things, done quietly

01 · Hide

The pull, removed

Each site loads in its own browser, with a script that hides Reels, Shorts, Explore and suggested posts as the page loads. Shorts links open as normal videos.

02 · Limit

A day that ends

Give each site a daily budget that resets at 6:00 am. When it’s used you see a calm screen, not a scolding. Extending takes a short wait and a typed phrase.

03 · Shield

The official apps, blocked

Screen Time shielding keeps the full apps out of reach. Need to post? Post Mode opens a short window for uploads.

No streaks. No shame. Just an end.

The time bar turns amber, never red. The message at the limit is three words. Nothing is counting how often you came back.
